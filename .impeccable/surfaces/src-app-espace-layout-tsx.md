# Application shell and work screens

Mode: Operate. The marketing team and client meetings (projected screen). Category standard chosen by the user, with Linear, Attio and Lighthouse as the craft bar. Light theme, discreet Sibuet touch.

## Direction contract

THESIS: a working app in the class of Linear and Attio, not an editorial site. Fixed sidebar, light canvas, white panels, dense tables. Refuses photo banners, serif titles, spaced capitals and editorial eyebrows.

OWN-WORLD: warm neutral canvas #f5f4f0, white panels with a 12 px radius and a 1 px line #e7e4de, ink #1c1c1a. Lodge Park navy #16213a for primary actions and the active item. Cognac #a8683f for "today" and the replay. States: sage, brick, ochre. Inter at 13-14 px with tabular figures. Lucide icons with a 1.6 stroke.

STORY: you open the app on "Cette semaine", read where the season stands, decide three priorities in place, then drill into a market or a sujet.

FIRST VIEWPORT: sidebar (logo, maison, market, navigation in three groups). Top bar (title, replay, actions). Row of season figures with a cumulative curve vs last year, fragile-lead alert, three priority cards with their buttons.

SIGNATURE: market purchase windows drawn as a timeline with a cognac "today" line, on the home page and the market pages.

RISK: leftover editorial styles in secondary components; sweep them.

seed: none (category standard chosen by the user)
